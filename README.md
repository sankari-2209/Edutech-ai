# EduTrack AI 🎓🤖

### AI-Powered Personalized Learning & Placement Preparation Platform

EduTrack AI is an AI-powered educational platform designed to make college learning more personalized, interactive, and effective. It helps students learn academic subjects, clear doubts, practice skills, prepare for placements, and track their daily learning progress.

## ✨ Features

* 📚 **Personalized Learning** – Learn college courses through modules and lessons.
* 🤖 **AI Tutor** – Ask doubts and get simple, beginner-friendly explanations.
* 🌐 **Multilingual AI** – Get explanations in English, Tamil, and Hindi.
* 🔥 **Learning Streaks** – Maintain daily learning streaks and receive reminders.
* 📝 **AI Summaries** – Quickly summarize lessons and study materials.
* 🧠 **AI Quizzes & Flashcards** – Automatically generate practice questions and revision cards.
* 📌 **Smart Notes** – Create and manage notes while learning.
* 🌎 **General Learning** – Learn any topic with AI beyond regular college courses.
* 💼 **Placement Preparation** – Prepare for aptitude, coding, communication, and interviews.
* 💻 **Coding Practice** – Practice C, C++, Java, Python, SQL, DSA, and algorithms.
* 🗣️ **Communication Training** – Improve GD, presentations, self-introduction, and professional communication.
* 👩‍🏫 **Teacher Dashboard** – Teachers can monitor student progress, scores, streaks, and weak areas.
* 📊 **AI Insights & Reports** – Generate useful learning insights and progress reports.

## 🎯 Problem We Solve

Students often struggle with finding personalized learning resources, clearing doubts, maintaining consistency, and preparing for placements while managing their college curriculum.

EduTrack AI brings **learning, AI assistance, progress tracking, and placement preparation into one platform**.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Student or Teacher logs into the platform.
2. Students access their courses and learning modules.
3. AI helps students understand lessons and clear doubts.
4. Students complete lessons and maintain their learning streak.
5. AI generates quizzes, summaries, and flashcards for revision.
6. Students practice aptitude, coding, communication, and interviews.
7. Teachers monitor student performance through the dashboard.
